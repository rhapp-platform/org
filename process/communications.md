# Communications Guide

## Customer-facing emails
- Send from `nano@rhappsody.com`
- BCC `glenn@rhappsody.com` on all customer-facing emails (policy #1)
- Tone: friendly, clear, non-technical unless the customer is technical
- Always acknowledge the issue before explaining or asking for anything

## Escalation
- `critical` / `high` priority tickets → immediate alert to Nano
- `normal` / `low` → batched triage summary
- See `support/tier-framework.md` for full priority rules

## Agent-to-agent
- Be concise — every message costs the reader's attention
- Lead with the outcome, not the process
- For long tasks: one ack at the start, one result at the end
